Our Story

Built by founders,
for founders.

PATH was founded in 2019 by a group of entrepreneurs who had spent a combined 40 years fighting against financial systems that weren't designed for them. Credit decisions made by algorithms trained on data from a different era. Banking products built for Main Street businesses, not hypergrowth startups. Venture debt with terms that favored lenders over founders.

We set out to build something different — a financial services company that treats every founder as a full partner. One that makes decisions with speed and transparency. One that offers products calibrated to the actual needs of companies at every stage, from first hire to first IPO.

Today PATH serves over 2,500 companies across 34 states. We've helped founders access more than $2B in capital, launch tens of thousands of accounts, and connect with the investors and advisors who helped them scale. We're just getting started.

Core Values

What drives us

Founder First

Every product decision, every policy, every hire is evaluated through one lens: does this make life better for the founders we serve? When in doubt, we choose the founder.

Radical Transparency

We believe founders deserve to know exactly why a decision was made — especially a no. Clear terms, honest timelines, and no hidden fees — ever.

Speed as Respect

Moving slowly costs founders money and momentum. We've engineered our processes around the principle that a fast, honest answer is almost always better than a slow, careful one.

Long-Term Thinking

We don't optimize for the transaction. We build relationships. Our best clients have been with us through multiple companies, and that loyalty is the metric we care most about.
